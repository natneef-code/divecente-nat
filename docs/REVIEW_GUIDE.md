# Review guide

## Try the slice
1. Open https://deploy-preview-1--divecente-nat.netlify.app (verified for Phase 2). Choose View Courses → Open Water.
2. Continue as Customer. Select a date and up to four fictional participants. Choose included equipment or an optional computer.
3. Review prerequisites, optionally submit placeholder documents, accept demo terms, choose QR and create the booking.
4. Select Simulate successful payment. One student without add-ons is THB 8,500 total, THB 850 deposit, THB 7,650 remaining.
5. Open My workspace to see the same booking after refresh.
6. Switch demo role → Front Desk. Confirm the booking, then check in after document acknowledgements are submitted.
7. Open Operations calendar; inspect the same students and capacity. Switch Instructor to inspect assigned roster without financial controls, or Manager for booking totals.
8. Repeat with Wise: submitted proof remains unpaid until Front Desk approves; rejection permits resubmission.

All four roles are available at /demo and /login without passwords. Stay in the same browser/profile for shared fictional records. Separate devices have independent data. No real reservation, payment, medical clearance, message or official certification is created.
